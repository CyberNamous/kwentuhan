A little clarity about your space.

Kwentuhan is an automated chat companion, not a person, therapist, or emergency service. It replies from a fixed set of topics, so it can miss what you mean.

On this device

Your conversation is stored in this browser’s LocalStorage, not in an app database. Anyone with access to this browser may see it. Private browsing or browser cleanup may erase it. End session removes Kwentuhan’s saved conversation and resets your preferences.

When you send a message

Your message and recent conversation are sent to this app’s backend on Cloudflare, which picks a reply from its built-in dataset. No AI model is used and no third-party AI service receives your messages. This app does not store conversation content on its server, but the hosting platform may keep standard request logs. Avoid sharing passwords, financial details, or identifying information.

Stopping or ending a session cancels the browser request. A reply that already started may not stop immediately. Ending a session cannot undo data already transmitted.

When you need a real person

If you’re in immediate danger in the Philippines, call 911. For crisis support, NCMH lists 1553 and 0917-899-8727. Reach out to someone you trust nearby, too.

Contacts checked 9 November 2026 against NCMH and the government hotline directory. Availability can change. Outside the Philippines, contact local emergency services.

Recent turns are used to follow the conversation; very long chats may not be remembered in full. Up to 100 messages are kept locally.
